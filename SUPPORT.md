# Support

- **Bugs and questions**: [GitHub issues](https://github.com/amiable-dev/chancery/issues). Include the `kb` command, its `--format json` output, and `node --version`.
- **Security**: never a public issue — see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Contributions**: read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) first; the PR contract is load-bearing.

Single-maintainer project: best-effort responses, no SLA. Acknowledgement targets are in SECURITY.md for vulnerabilities only.
