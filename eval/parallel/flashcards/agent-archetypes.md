# Agent archetypes — Flashcards

#flashcards/agents

## Organizing axis: purpose, not build <!-- kb:card:ac4753 -->
What is the organizing axis for the agent-archetypes taxonomy of compound AI system workloads?
?
What each workload shape is for — sorted by breadth/depth of judgment, convergence, meta-observation, routing, or closed-loop control — rather than by how the agent is built.

## Worker archetype <!-- kb:card:68b95a -->
What is the 'worker' archetype, and what previously blocked this kind of work from being automated?
?
Applying a little judgment to an enormous number of near-identical items (e.g. inspecting every cluster or server against a spec); previously automatable only where the difference between items could be written as an if-then rule.

## Ruminative agent <!-- kb:card:2fc988 -->
How does the ruminative agent archetype's resource profile differ from the worker's, and how is it often structured?
?
Inverted economics: few inputs but long reasoning — often several agents each holding a different objective (security, cost, latency) arguing it out as a structured debate rather than answering once.

## Consultant archetype <!-- kb:card:0c9fdf -->
What does the 'consultant' archetype do, and why does that matter for keeping a system efficient?
?
It is the meta-observer that watches the system's own runs for behaviour repetitive enough to demote to a fixed rule — the mechanism for continuously applying the determinism split rather than deciding it once at design time.

## Director archetype is aspirational <!-- kb:card:60d2f4 -->
What is the status of the 'director' archetype (closing an OODA loop over a constrained domain), per the source?
?
Explicitly aspirational — the speaker states nobody is running one unattended, and expects it first over narrow domains as evals mature.
