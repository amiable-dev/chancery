# Datasheets for datasets — Flashcards

#flashcards/data-governance

## Definition <!-- kb:card:a6408d -->
What is the 'datasheets for datasets' practice?
?
Every dataset ships with a structured datasheet answering fixed questions about its motivation, composition, collection process, preprocessing, recommended uses, distribution and maintenance — by analogy with electronics components, which ship with datasheets too.

## Key mechanism <!-- kb:card:dfadff -->
How does a fixed-question datasheet act as a forcing function?
?
Because the categories are fixed, creators must surface decisions that would otherwise stay tribal knowledge (why data was collected, what's in or missing, how it was gathered), and the answers travel with the data so consumers can judge fitness for purpose without contacting the producer.

## Fixed question categories <!-- kb:card:1a1be3 -->
What are the fixed question categories a dataset datasheet must answer?
?
Motivation, composition, collection process, preprocessing and labeling, recommended uses, distribution, and maintenance.

## Electronics analogy <!-- kb:card:e61b39 -->
What industry practice inspired the 'datasheet' name for datasets?
?
Electronics components, however simple, ship with a datasheet stating operating characteristics, test results and recommended uses — the proposal applies the same idea to datasets.

## Nature of the source <!-- kb:card:b617d2 -->
What kind of evidence does the original datasheets-for-datasets proposal rest on?
?
It's a position paper (Gebru et al., arXiv 2018 / CACM 2021) arguing by analogy and stated goals, not a measured empirical study of the practice's effect.
