# Dispositions — what was done with every review finding

Council output is judgment, not corroboration (ADR-010): each finding was verified against the code, the invariants, and the corpus before adoption. Finding numbers refer to the packet synthesis artifacts in this directory.

**The `status`/`council_review` lifecycle rule** (resolves packet-1 finding ADR-001.5 and packet-3 C7, once, here): `status: accepted` records the **owner's decision**; `council_review` records the **quality gate** separately — date, verdict, and a pointer to this file. An ADR may be accepted before review (the decision was the owner's to make) but may not enter the build phase until its review verdict is incorporated or dispositioned. All sixteen documents in this review set now carry a reviewed header; none cites council output as *evidence* for its own decision — reviews are named as artifacts, and the standing rule that panel agreement corroborates nothing appears in ADR-010.
