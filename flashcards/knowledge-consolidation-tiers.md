# Knowledge Consolidation Tiers — Flashcards

#flashcards/knowledge-management


## Definition <!-- kb:card:5905ae -->
What are the four tiers of knowledge consolidation?
?
1. **Working memory** — recent raw observations, unprocessed (hours to days).
2. **Episodic memory** — session summaries compressed from raw observations (days to weeks).
3. **Semantic memory** — cross-session facts consolidated from episodes (months to years).
4. **Procedural memory** — workflows and patterns extracted from repeated semantics (persistent).

## Application <!-- kb:card:196f08 -->
When would you use a tiered consolidation pipeline?
?
When building an AI knowledge base that needs to scale beyond a few sessions. Tiers prevent noise accumulation (working memory decays fast) while preserving hard-won insights (procedural memory persists indefinitely). Essential when a flat MEMORY.md starts to blur recent observations with stable long-term facts.

## Relationship <!-- kb:card:85537a -->
How do Knowledge Consolidation Tiers relate to Knowledge Crystallisation?
?
Crystallisation is the automated mechanism for promoting a completed work chain from episodic to semantic memory. It distils a finished exploration into a structured digest, which is then filed as a semantic-tier page.

## Promotion <!-- kb:card:bf042e -->
What drives promotion from one tier to the next?
?
Accumulating evidence: a fact seen once stays in working memory; the same pattern across multiple sessions promotes to episodic; cross-session convergence promotes to semantic; repeated operational use promotes to procedural. Promotion is evidence-driven, not time-driven.
