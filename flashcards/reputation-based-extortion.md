# Reputation-Based Extortion — Flashcards

#flashcards/security

## Definition <!-- kb:card:b0c473 -->
What is reputation-based extortion?
?
An attack strategy where cybercriminals pressure a victim into paying by threatening damage to brand reputation, public trust, or customer relationships — rather than (or alongside) technical disruption like encryption. It adds a lever that good backups cannot neutralise.

## Application <!-- kb:card:554629 -->
When would you use the concept of reputation-based extortion in incident response planning?
?
When building runbooks or tabletop exercises: treat "we restored from backup" as necessary but not sufficient. Plan a parallel disclosure/PR-pressure response track, since the reputational lever survives a clean technical recovery.

## Relationship <!-- kb:card:a56c22 -->
How does reputation-based extortion relate to zero trust architecture?
?
IBM's 2026 Cost of a Data Breach report recommends zero trust's "trusted identity controls for users, data and machine agents" as the mitigation — reducing the chance sensitive data reaches an exfiltration point that attackers could later threaten to expose.

## Key Stat <!-- kb:card:611ace -->
What share of ransomware victims reported attackers using reputation-damage threats, per IBM's 2026 report?
?
41% of ransomware victims, based on a sample of 602 organisations breached between March 2025 and February 2026.
