# Fable 5 export-control directive — Flashcards

#flashcards/governance

## Definition <!-- kb:card:48410e -->
What was the Fable 5 export-control directive?
?
An August 2026 US government order under national-security authorities suspending all foreign-national access to Fable 5 and Mythos 5, which in effect forced the models offline for all customers -- the first government recall of a deployed frontier model.

## Stated basis vs Anthropic's review <!-- kb:card:226ce4 -->
What was the government's stated basis for the directive, and what did Anthropic's review find?
?
A demonstrated jailbreak; Anthropic's review found it narrow and non-universal, eliciting a capability (reading and fixing flaws in a codebase) it judged widely available in other deployed models.

## Universal vs non-universal jailbreak <!-- kb:card:683ea4 -->
What is the distinction between a universal and a non-universal jailbreak, per this directive's framing?
?
A universal jailbreak broadly bypasses safeguards across a wide capability range; a non-universal jailbreak elicits specific information only in specific circumstances.

## Defense in depth <!-- kb:card:af47cd -->
What deployment posture does the company defend, given that perfect jailbreak resistance is asserted to be impossible?
?
Defense in depth: make jailbreaks narrow or expensive, then monitor, detect, and shut down, backed by a 30-day data-retention policy adopted for jailbreak research.

## Governance position <!-- kb:card:2e1967 -->
What kind of process does the company argue should govern any government blocking of unsafe AI deployments?
?
A transparent, fair, clear, and technically grounded statutory process, which it argues this particular directive did not meet.
