# TREC (Text REtrieval Conference) — Flashcards

#flashcards/evaluation

## TREC: sponsors and start <!-- kb:card:836839 -->
Who runs TREC, and since what year?
?
NIST and the U.S. Department of Defense, running since 1992, originating from the TIPSTER program.

## TREC's relation to Cranfield <!-- kb:card:8ad5fb -->
What did TREC do to the Cranfield paradigm?
?
It scaled the paradigm into shared infrastructure — large standard test collections and an open forum spanning academia, industry, and government, enabling comparable large-scale evaluations.

## Stated goals <!-- kb:card:26e3f4 -->
What are TREC's three stated infrastructural goals?
?
Encourage research on large shared collections, increase cross-sector communication, and speed technology transfer by demonstrating measured improvements at real scale.

## The durable institutional lesson <!-- kb:card:55b57a -->
What is the durable lesson of TREC's institutional design?
?
Retrieval research improved when evaluation became common property: one collection, one query set, one set of judgments, many competing systems, results comparable by construction.

## Track structure <!-- kb:card:3468dc -->
What does TREC's track structure let the institution do?
?
Re-apply the same evaluation machinery to new retrieval problems as they emerge.

## Recorded gap <!-- kb:card:0d0b97 -->
What operationally important TREC methodology is flagged in this note as a recorded gap rather than covered in depth?
?
Its pooled relevance-judging process across participating systems and the qrels format — detail one level deeper than the cited institutional overview.
