# Microcopy doubt injection — Flashcards

#flashcards/ux-writing

## Microcopy doubt injection — definition <!-- kb:card:81a72c -->
What is microcopy doubt injection?
?
The failure mode where small, operationally accurate phrases placed at high-stakes moments (fees, approvals, timing, consent) create hesitation disproportionate to the fact they state, because a reader at the point of commitment is scanning for reasons to stop.

## Doubt injection — why placement matters <!-- kb:card:5e9f87 -->
Why does a phrase like 'processing fees may apply' matter more in one location than another?
?
Because damage scales with position — the phrase is harmless in help text but costly next to a payment button, where the reader is scanning for reasons to stop.

## Doubt injection — phrase families <!-- kb:card:5df008 -->
Name three of the recognizable 'doubt families' that risky microcopy phrases cluster into.
?
Hidden-cost suspicion (e.g. 'processing fees'), rejection fear (e.g. 'subject to approval'), and delay anxiety (e.g. 'please allow up to'). Also: effort anxiety, privacy concern, and legalistic friction.

## Doubt injection audit constraints <!-- kb:card:da7ec6 -->
What two constraints keep a microcopy doubt audit honest — one about required disclosures, one about replacement copy?
?
Required disclosures must be made clearer and more reassuring, never deleted; and no replacement may invent a guarantee, timeline, fee, or policy the business doesn't actually offer.

## Doubt injection — evidence status <!-- kb:card:247e4f -->
What is the evidentiary status of the severity rankings in a microcopy doubt audit?
?
They are heuristics, not measured findings — the source reports no measured conversion lift, so rankings await A/B testing.
