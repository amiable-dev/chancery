# Goodhart's law variants — Flashcards

#flashcards/evaluation

## Goodhart's law variants: authorship <!-- kb:card:d95e30 -->
Who formalized Goodhart's law into distinct variants, and in what year?
?
Manheim & Garrabrant, 2018, building on Garrabrant's earlier taxonomy.

## How many mechanisms <!-- kb:card:abd26c -->
How many distinct mechanisms does the Goodhart's law variants taxonomy identify?
?
At least four distinct mechanisms.

## Central thesis <!-- kb:card:1d012b -->
What is the paper's central thesis about metric overoptimization?
?
It is not one failure but a family: the umbrella term 'Goodhart's law' hides mechanically different ways a metric-goal relationship breaks under optimization pressure.

## Where severity is greatest <!-- kb:card:e91eda -->
Where is Goodhart failure severity greatest, and why?
?
Machine learning and AI alignment, because failure severity scales with the optimization power directed at the proxy.

## Correct diagnosis <!-- kb:card:253b60 -->
What does correctly diagnosing a Goodhart failure require, per this taxonomy?
?
Naming the specific variant/mechanism at play, not citing the umbrella law — 'is this Goodhartable?' is four separate questions, not one.
