# Knowledge Crystallisation — Flashcards

#flashcards/knowledge-management


## Definition <!-- kb:card:17d859 -->
What is Knowledge Crystallisation?
?
The automated process of taking a completed work chain (research thread, debugging session, analysis arc) and distilling it into a structured digest: question, findings, entities involved, decisions, and lessons. The digest becomes a first-class wiki page; extracted lessons feed back into the fact store.

## Application <!-- kb:card:c444fb -->
Why treat your own exploration sessions as knowledge sources?
?
Debugging sessions reveal architectural insights; research threads produce validated conclusions; analysis sessions identify patterns. These are never recaptured if only filed as raw logs. Crystallisation turns every completed exploration into institutional memory without manual bookkeeping overhead.

## Relationship <!-- kb:card:c014a3 -->
How does Knowledge Crystallisation relate to the Knowledge Consolidation Tiers?
?
Crystallisation is the mechanism for the episodic→semantic tier promotion. A completed work chain lives in episodic memory as raw session notes; crystallisation compresses it into a structured digest that enters semantic memory as a stable, citable page.

## Structured digest <!-- kb:card:6555be -->
What are the five components of a crystallisation digest?
?
1. **Question** — the triggering problem or goal.
2. **Findings** — what was discovered.
3. **Entities** — projects/services/people/files involved.
4. **Decisions** — what was decided and why.
5. **Lessons** — what generalises beyond this specific case.
