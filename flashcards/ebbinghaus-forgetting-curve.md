# Ebbinghaus forgetting curve — Flashcards

#flashcards/knowledge-management

## Definition <!-- kb:card:4e5570 -->
What is the Ebbinghaus forgetting curve?
?
The empirical finding, first measured by Hermann Ebbinghaus (1885) via self-experiments on nonsense syllables, that memory retention decays steeply with time when no attempt is made to retain it — newly learned material roughly halves within days or weeks absent review.

## Key mechanism <!-- kb:card:8bb672 -->
How did Ebbinghaus quantify retention in his experiments?
?
As 'savings' — the reduction in time needed to relearn material compared with the time needed to learn it the first time — fitted with a logarithmic decay formula.

## Shape of the curve <!-- kb:card:43e7ae -->
What shape does retention loss take over time, per the Ebbinghaus curve?
?
Steepest immediately after learning, then flattening out over time.

## Replication <!-- kb:card:e16756 -->
How did a 2015 replication of Ebbinghaus's 1885 self-experiment turn out?
?
It reproduced results similar to the original data — notable durability for a 140-year-old self-experiment.

## Scope of the original finding <!-- kb:card:9376cd -->
Did Ebbinghaus's original work study the effect of spaced review, or only the forgetting itself?
?
Only the forgetting itself. Spaced repetition — scheduling review at increasing intervals to counteract the curve — is a derivative practice that came later, built on his curve as its empirical foundation.
